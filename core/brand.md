# Bumpy brand rules

The rules every creative follows, whoever makes it and with whatever tool. One home: the brainstormer, the brief
writer and any other tool read this file instead of keeping their own copy. Changes: the creative lead (log them in
`core/decisions.md`). Merged on 3 Nov 2026 from the strategists' `bumpy-knowledge.md` v1.1, `bumpy-learnings.md`,
`bumpy-brief-writer` v5 and `PROJECT.md`.

## What Bumpy is
An international dating and social app. Cross-border matching is the product: people from different countries
connect. Distance is the feature, not a bug. The app never changes between personas; only the door does (dating,
friends, language, faith…), and the real desire behind every door is dating.

## Hard rules
1. **Never "nearby", "near you", "in your area"** or any local-radius word in copy or briefs. Use a distance word:
   abroad, from another country, worldwide, a country name or a flag. When a strategist's own line uses "nearby"
   contrastively ("boys only date nearby, men date abroad"), flag it and let them decide.
2. **Russia (RU) is excluded** from casting, faces, locations and copy. Bumpy is Ukrainian-founded. "Slavic" casting
   means UA and similar, never RU.
3. **India (IN) is excluded from casting** (board decision: already over-represented, not a growth target).
4. **Map and UI frames:** check visible pins and profiles before shipping. No India or Russia in frame.
5. **One persona per creative.** Never mix audiences in one ad. The same creative used for another persona gets its
   own file name for that persona (`core/naming-ad-v5.md`).
6. **Only `LocalDatingFatigue` may name competitor apps** (Tinder, Hinge, Bumble) as the villain. Never for any other
   persona. Its male pain (zero matches) and female pain (numbed by volume) are separate scripts, never merged.
7. **Persona names** come only from `core/naming-adset-v8.md`. Never invent one; if a creative fits none or two, ask.
8. **Non-native languages** (Turkish especially): mark lines for a native check before shipping.
9. **Claims need a source.** "#1" claims may be rejected by Meta without one. Numbers in use that still need checking
   before they go in public ads:
   - US men match about 20% of the time on Bumpy vs about 5% on local apps (CEO figure). Confirm Bumpy's calculation
     (matches ÷ likes sent?) first.
   - Top 5 countries for US men (team ranking, Sep 2026): PH, BR, ID, CU, CO.

## Endscreen CTA: confirm per persona
Always the existing endscreen visual (brand template, not the in-app UI) plus the CTA. Ask which CTA applies; never
assume. Localise it for non-English variants and give the English back-translation.

| Persona | CTA |
|---|---|
| Default | `Find your international soulmate. Bumpy.` (ES: `Encuentra a tu alma gemela internacional`) |
| ChristianMatchWorldwide | `Meet Christian Singles Worldwide` |
| LatinaFemaleLover | `Find Latina Worldwide` |
| InternationalFriendFinder | `Find your international friends` |
| NewLanguageLearner | `Date A Native Speaker` |
| Turkish default | `Uluslararası ruh eşini bul. Bumpy.` (native check) |

Wonder (sister brand, gay international dating, same workflow): `Meet gay people worldwide`
(PT: `Conheça pessoas gays do mundo todo`).

## Looks real, not AI
Production lesson from shipped briefs: creatives must look like real TikTok content. Natural, dim or warm lighting;
avoid white walls; always tweak the talent's face from the reference; match a benchmark's vibe but change at least one
element so it looks similar side by side, not identical.
