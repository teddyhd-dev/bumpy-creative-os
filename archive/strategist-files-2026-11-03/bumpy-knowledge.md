## What Bumpy Is

An international dating and social app. The core mechanic is **cross-border matching** — people from completely different countries connecting. Distance is the feature, not a bug.

Top performing country pairs by data:
- US ↔ Philippines
- US ↔ Brazil
- US ↔ Turkey
- US ↔ Thailand
- US ↔ Indonesia
- UK ↔ Indonesia
- UK ↔ Morocco
- Western Europe ↔ Southeast Asia

**NEVER use "nearby" or "near you" in copy.** This is not a local dating app. The international gap is the whole product.

## Brand Rules — Non-Negotiable

**Endscreen CTA — confirm per persona.** The default is `Find your international soulmate. Bumpy.` Some personas use their own locked CTA (e.g. `Meet Christian Singles Worldwide`, `Find Latina Worldwide`, `Find your international friends`). Always confirm which one applies to the brief, and localize it for non-English variants.

**Russia (RU) excluded from all casting and copy.** Bumpy is a Ukrainian-founded company. Never cast Russian profiles, faces, or locations in any creative. If RU appears in attractiveness data, skip it and use the next best option.

**India (IN) excluded from all casting.** Board decision — India is already over-represented in the user base and is not a growth target. Never cast Indian profiles in ads.

## The Core Insight: It's Always a Pure Angle Play

The app itself doesn't change. Every persona is matched to the same product. What changes is the angle — who you're talking to, what desire you activate, what objection you kill.

This means:
- One persona = one angle = one set of hooks
- Never mix personas in one creative
- The app can be "dating", "friends", "language learning" — same product, different door

## Proven Headline Formulas (Extract From Working Ads)

### Formula 1: "More than you think" — kills the scarcity objection
> "There are more [foreign men] wanting to date [nationality] women than you think"

Works because the #1 objection is always: "nobody like me wants someone like me." This kills it in one line.

### Formula 2: Country flag + nationality specificity
> "Highly recommend falling in love with a 🇮🇹 man on Bumpy"

Flags are visual shorthand. Specific nationality beats vague "international" every time.

### Formula 3: POV discovery moment
> "POV: you didn't realize guys abroad are lining up for Filipinas on Bumpy"

Written as the viewer's own realization. Not about them — as them.

### Formula 4: The two-slide turn ("fun… until")
Slide 1: relatable setup
Slide 2: surprising/funny payoff

> "Chatting with a foreigner is fun… until"
> "He booked a flight to come see you ❤️"

The gap between slide 1 and 2 is where the emotion lives.

### Formula 5: Social proof timeline
> "February 2025 — Matched with a British Muslim guy on Bumpy"
> "February 2026 — Are you free this Valentine's?"
> "He came to see me"

Real-feeling timeline = credibility. Works especially for hesitant personas.

### Formula 6: Outcome statement with flag
> "I'm moving to my new bf's country 🇸🇪 3 months after using Bumpy"

The flag makes it specific. The timeline makes it believable.

### Formula 7: Paradox-authority hook (from "certified mindfucker" signal, Aug 2026)
> "How to fix zero matches. From a guy with a 0% match rate on Tinder."
> "How to stop expecting nothing. From a girl with 400 unread 'hey's."

Credibility comes FROM the failure/pain, not despite it — the "expert" is proof the system was broken. Paired with a quick-cut montage of cut-off example lines ("scroll" transitions) that demonstrate the pain live before the reveal. Closes with a self-aware turn that reframes the pain as a market/algorithm problem, not a personal one, then positions Bumpy as the exit. See "Dating App Refugee" persona below for full scripts. **Superseded for the male sub-angle by Formula 8 below — kept here for reference / possible female-side use.**

### Formula 8: "CTE broken-grammar" myth-break (from user-supplied benchmark series, Aug 2026)
Source benchmark: a recurring fitness-coach TikTok series titled "[Topic] but we both have CTE 🧠" — deliberately broken, telegraphic grammar (dropped articles/auxiliaries, "?" used as an if/when conditional shorthand, short chained cause-effect clauses). The joke is a fictional brain injury; the effect is that dense information reads as radically simple and impossible to spin.

**This is now the primary format for the Dating App Refugee male sub-angle.** Structure:
1. Blunt opener naming the exact pain, phrased as a diagnosis, not a question to ponder
2. State the app's own marketing promise, then kill it in one word: `"[promise]." Lie.`
3. Name the real mechanism plainly (a "celebrity like" tier system — top profiles get pushed to everyone, everyone else gets buried)
4. A deliberately half-contradictory turn: "Your fault? Not really. Your fault is limiting your options to small pool." — reassures (not your fault) and activates agency (but you can act) in the same breath. Logical tension here is intentional, not a flaw.
5. Small pool vs. big pool contrast, world numbers as the payoff (`0.00001%` seen vs `99.999%` shown by Bumpy)
6. Two-line close: kill the belief, then the CTA — `Stop believe dating app promise. Download Bumpy. Match today.`

No montage, no "from a certified X" framing, no explainer sentences — every line is a fact or a consequence, nothing is there to transition or soften. See flagship scripts below.

## NEW ANGLE: Dating App Refugee / Local Dating Fatigue (added Aug 2026)

**Who:** Active or recently-churned users of Tinder, Hinge, Bumble and similar *local* dating apps. Unlike every other persona above, this one is defined in opposition to competitor apps directly — the "normal" radius-limited dating app is the villain, Bumpy is the escape. This is deliberate, not accidental: we are not "a dating app," we are the international alternative to the local ones.

**Core mechanism (use in body copy, any sub-angle):**
- Local apps cap you by radius — you're fighting over the same recycled pool
- Local apps' matching algorithm concentrates attention on a tiny top slice of profiles (the "0.00001%") and buries almost everyone else
- Beauty/desirability is not fixed — it's culturally relative. Someone ranked "mid" by one market's algorithm can be exactly someone's type in another country
- Bumpy removes the radius cap and boosts your profile worldwide — showing you (and showing you to) the other 99.9999%, not just the local top sliver

**The pain splits by gender — do not merge into one script.**

## Key Rules for Writing Bumpy Copy

1. **Never "nearby" or "near you"** — cross-border distance is the product
2. **Flags > words** — 🇧🇷🇹🇭🇮🇩🇵🇭 are faster than "Brazilian" or "Filipino"
3. **Specificity beats "international"** — name the nationality, the country, the language (on-screen hooks; for Meta headlines see `bumpy-learnings.md`, where abstract lines outperformed)
4. **One persona = one creative** — never mix audiences
5. **The desire is always the real desire** — friends angle, language angle, Christian angle — the hook is always a door to dating
6. **"More than you think" kills every scarcity objection** — use it when the persona doubts they're wanted
7. **The two-slide turn is proven** — setup (relatable) → payoff (surprising/romantic outcome)
8. **Local language copy outperforms English** for non-English personas (Thai, Indonesian, Portuguese, Spanish)
9. **Endscreen CTA is confirmed per persona.** Default `Find your international soulmate. Bumpy.`; persona-specific CTAs exist (see Brand Rules)
10. **Never cast RU (Russia) or IN (India)** in any creative — see Brand Rules above
11. **Dating App Refugee persona names competitors directly** (Tinder/Hinge/Bumble) — this is the one persona allowed to demonize another dating app category. Never do this for any other persona.
12. **Man vs. woman churn pain are different scripts** — zero matches (male) vs. numbed-by-volume (female). Never merge them into one script.
13. **For Dating App Refugee (male), use the CTE-style broken-grammar format (Formula 8)** — not the paradox-authority format. No montage, no explainer sentences, no borrowed reaction lines — every line is a fact, a promise-kill, or a number.

## Standard Scene Types (App UI & Creative Scenes)

These are reusable scene building blocks. Designers recognize them by name. Full library and named sequences live in `bumpy-brief-brainstorm-SKILL.md`; gen-task math lives in `bumpy-brief-writer-SKILL.md`.

### [MAP SWIPE]
Bumpy's world map UI. Profile photos pinned across the globe with country flag badges — shows users from different countries worldwide, not nearby. Bumpy logo visible in center. Existing template asset — no generation needed. Check the frame shows no India/Russia pins.
- ✅ Correct: "people across the world on Bumpy"
- ❌ Wrong: "people nearby" — Bumpy is international, not local

### [SLIDER] / [PROFILES]
Horizontal swipe of profile cards (standard 4). Each card: profile photo + first name + country flag. Specify nationality and gender. Images only. Always has on-screen text.

### [VIDEO CALL]
In-app video call, split screen. Specify who is large (main view) and who is small (secondary). Format: `[Large: Nationality Gender] / [Small: Nationality Gender]`.

### [MANY DM]
Scrolling DM inbox full of unread messages from multiple users worldwide. Shows volume of interest. Specify account holder gender/ethnicity and visible sender nationalities.

### [CHAT]
In-app chat conversation. Messages pop up one at a time. **This is an After Effects template — designer edits messages directly in AE. Not AI generated. Gen: 0.**
Specify: exact messages in order only. No image generation needed.

### [TRAVEL]
Two people together in a real-world location. Couple-feeling, natural. Specify: both people's nationality + gender, city/country, landmark if relevant.

### [ITS A BUMP]
Bumpy's equivalent of "It's a Match" — existing UI animation. Appears after [PROFILES]. Very short (~1 second). **No on-screen text — never assign copy to this scene.** Gen: 0.

### [AIRPORT]
Two people meeting or reuniting at an airport. Emotional, couple energy. ~2 seconds. Gen: 1 image + 1 image-to-video per variant.

### [EATING]
Couple eating local food together in a specific location. ~2 seconds. Gen: 1 image + 1 image-to-video per variant.

### [CAR]
Couple in a car together, candid, intimate. ~2 seconds. Gen: 1 image + 1 image-to-video per variant.

### [PROPOSAL]
One person proposing to the other. High emotional payoff. Can be hook or final scene. Gen: 1 image + 1 image-to-video per variant.

### [ENDSCREEN]
The existing endscreen visual (brand template, not the in-app UI) + CTA text. Always final scene. Never counted in gen tasks.
**CTA: confirm per persona.** Default `Find your international soulmate. Bumpy.`
