1 · What the switch moved, and what it didn't

Each metric against its own Dec – mid-Feb average (1.00), by OS

Dotted lines: the switch week of each OS. Numbers credited through the MMP (ROAS, purchases, installs, click → install) stepped down in the switch week. CPM didn't. iOS click-through also jumped in the switch week while click → install halved: the iOS click path changed with the switch (link, landing page or how clicks are counted), so iOS CTR and click → install are not comparable across it either. Hook rate also fell, starting the week before the switch; Meta counts it itself, so that is the creative mix, not the ruler.
