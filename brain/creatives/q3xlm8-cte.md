# Q3xLm8 · cte

## Decode
- First second: on-screen text in deliberately broken grammar, e.g. "You handsome. Zero match? Something wrong."
  (variants: "Swipe all day, get nothing? Bad." · "You lonely long time, dating app no help? Time know why.")
- Hook: names the exact pain as a diagnosis, in telegraphic "we both have CTE" grammar, so dense information reads
  radically simple.
- Message: dating apps lie ("thousands of people near you"); they push a few "celebrity like" profiles and bury the
  rest. Not your fault; your fault is staying in a small pool. Bumpy shows you the other 99.999%.
- Format: text-led myth-break, matched device for device to a fitness-coach TikTok series "[Topic] but we both have
  CTE 🧠" (dropped articles, "?" as an if/when shorthand, short chained cause → effect clauses).
- Why it stops the thumb: it never asks the viewer to trust an assertion. Every line is the app's own promise
  (killed in one word: "Lie."), a plain mechanism, or a number. "Your fault? Not really. Your fault is limiting your
  options to small pool." removes shame and hands back agency in one breath (deliberately loose logic, accepted).
- Full breakdown: scripts below. Skeleton: opener → myth-break → "celebrity like" mechanism → reassurance + agency
  turn → small pool vs big pool → 0.00001% vs 99.999% → kill the belief, then the CTA.

```
You handsome. Zero match? Something wrong.

Different eyes, different taste, different odds.
Buried here? Maybe wanted there.

You only see 0.00001% of your options.
Bumpy show you the 99.999%.

Stop believe dating app promise.
Download Bumpy. Match today.
```
**Version 2, "Swipe all day":** opener "Swipe all day, get nothing? Bad." and promise "so many matches waiting." Lie.;
close "Stop swiping dead promise. Download Bumpy. Match today." The middle is the same as version 1.

**Version 3, "Lonely long time":** opener "You lonely long time, dating app no help? Time know why." and promise "your
person out there." True. But not where they show you.; close "Stop lonely in wrong pool. Download Bumpy today." The
middle is the same as version 1.

(The quoted "near you" is the competitor's promise being killed, the one place it's allowed: `core/brand.md` rule 1.)
