# Options: from signal or ticket to scene-by-scene concepts

Used in SKILL.md step 4. Merged on 3 Nov 2026 from the strategists' `bumpy-brief-brainstorm` v2.1.0 (signal →
options, scene library, clarity rules), the scene types in `bumpy-knowledge.md` v1.1 and the brainstormer's own
option format (playbook move, evidence, hypothesis, odds, cost). Originals in `archive/strategist-files-2026-11-03/`.

## The one job
The viewer has **zero knowledge of Bumpy**, is mid-scroll and gives you 1.5 seconds. Every option passes: *would a
complete stranger understand what's happening and feel something in under 2 seconds?* If not, rewrite it.

## Two ways in, one format out
- **From a signal** (the strategist shares a post, ad or screenshot): decode it first (`craft/decode.md`), then
  give **3 options per signal**:
  - **A**: the closest literal adaptation. Same format, same visual energy, minimum distance from the signal.
  - **B**: same emotion, a slightly different structure. Still clearly from the same signal.
  - **C**: a creative leap rooted in the signal's exact emotion. Bolder, but traceable to what made the signal
    work. Not a new concept, not an invention.

  Stay close to the signal: its format, energy and emotion are why it works. **If an option could exist without
  having seen the signal, it's wrong.**
- **From a ticket** (no signal yet): 3–5 directions, each tagged with a playbook move (`playbook.md`), at least one
  safe (same message, new visual) and one learning option (new angle). Then ask for inspiration (SKILL.md step 5)
  and turn the chosen direction into A/B/C if a signal comes in.

Every option, either way, carries:
- **Scenes:** each scene with its type, a specific visual and the exact on-screen text (format below);
- **Headline** in the audience's language plus an English gloss (`craft/headlines.md`);
- **Evidence** behind it (adset, week, share; a past winner; a signal's numbers) and a **one-line hypothesis**;
- **Rough odds** from history (the base rate is about 0.3 for a new #1 within 4 weeks), **production cost**
  (generations, `craft/brief-writer.md` task weight) and **how many variants** it can yield.
Say which one you would pick and why.

```
### Option A — <2–3 word concept name>   · move: <playbook move>

Scene 1 — [HOOK-SELFIE]:
Visual: <specific, not vague>
Text: `<exact on-screen copy>`

Scene 2 — [PROFILES]:
Visual: <specific>
Text: `<exact on-screen copy>`

[only as many scenes as the story needs]

Hypothesis: … · Evidence: … · Odds: … · Cost: … · Variants: …
```

## Scene count follows the story
No fixed number; each scene moves the story exactly one step, or it goes.
- **1 scene:** the signal implies Bumpy so directly that one visual + one line closes it.
- **2 scenes:** hook (pure relatable, no brand) → redirect (Bumpy as the answer).
- **3 scenes:** hook → bridge → resolution.
- **4+ scenes:** hook → bridge → a sequence (proof, profiles, travel, payoff).
Smaller conversion gap = fewer scenes. If removing a scene doesn't break the story, remove it.

## Scene library (lego pieces)
Mix by what the story needs; never default to a sequence.

**Hook scenes** (organic-feeling, no Bumpy branding)
- `[HOOK-WALK]` walking feet or full body, airport or street, candid
- `[HOOK-SELFIE]` talking to camera in a car, room or plane: confessional
- `[HOOK-PLANE]` window seat, coffee, looking out
- `[HOOK-BALCONY]` staring out from a hotel or apartment balcony
- `[HOOK-POOL]` a filthy green pool: visual metaphor for the local dating scene
- `[HOOK-POV]` feet-first walking POV, face not shown
- New hook types are fine: name and describe them clearly.

**Bumpy UI scenes**
- `[MAP SWIPE]` world map swiping through countries, profile photos with flag badges pop up; Bumpy logo in the
  centre. Existing template, no generation. Shows people across the world, never "nearby". Check the pins: no
  India or Russia (`core/brand.md`).
- `[PROFILES]` / `[SLIDER]` horizontal swipe of profile cards (standard 4): photo + first name + flag. Specify
  gender and nationality (`craft/casting.md`). Always has on-screen text (e.g. `Women on Bumpy 🥵 >>>`).
- `[ITS A BUMP]` Bumpy's "It's a match" animation after the profiles. About 1 second. **Never any text.** 0 gen.
- `[CHAT]` messages popping in one at a time. After Effects template: list the exact messages in order. 0 gen.
- `[MANY DM]` a scrolling inbox of unread messages from many countries. Specify the account holder and the
  visible sender nationalities.
- `[VIDEO CALL]` split-screen in-app call: `[Large: nationality gender] / [Small: nationality gender]`.

**Payoff scenes** (about 2 seconds each; one shared payoff line, e.g. `Try international dating, you won't regret it`)
- `[AIRPORT]` two people meeting or reuniting · `[EATING]` a couple eating local food in a named place ·
  `[CAR]` a couple in a car, candid · `[TRAVEL]` two people at a real landmark or local setting · `[PROPOSAL]` a
  proposal: high payoff, can also be the hook.

**End**
- `[ENDSCREEN]` the existing endscreen visual (brand template, not app UI) + the CTA confirmed for this persona
  (`core/brand.md`). Always last. Never counted in generation.

**Named sequences** (drop in as a block only when the story calls for it; any piece can move or go):
- Love story: `[PROFILES] → [ITS A BUMP] → [CHAT] → [AIRPORT] → [EATING] → [CAR] → [PROPOSAL] → [ENDSCREEN]`
- Map discovery: `[MAP SWIPE] → [PROFILES] → [ITS A BUMP] → [CHAT] → [ENDSCREEN]`
- Quick proof: `[PROFILES] → [ITS A BUMP] → [CHAT] → [ENDSCREEN]`

## Hard rules
**Clarity**
- Zero Bumpy knowledge assumed: the redirect says what Bumpy is and does, cold. "They're already on Bumpy" fails;
  "Date someone from another country. Bumpy." works.
- 7th-grader test: read every line out loud. A stranger gets it at once, or rewrite.
- Logic-check every transition and every line. Rejected example: "Now I'm flying to 🇸🇪 to meet someone I met on
  there." It doesn't follow.
- One idea per scene. Two scenes with the same text are one scene.

**On-screen lines**
- 4–8 words per scene line; the hook headline 4–6 (`craft/headlines.md`). Short and striking beats long and
  high-context.
- Specificity converts: name a country, use a flag. 🇧🇷 beats "abroad". (Meta headlines differ: `playbook.md`.)
- No vague verbs: "Date someone from another country" beats "go international". "International" is not a verb.

**Visuals**
- Scene 1 is organic: no logo, no brand colours, no polish. Raw = trusted.
- Match the signal's energy exactly: walking → walking, car selfie → car selfie. Never upgrade the hook's production.
- Visual and text amplify each other: not the same thing twice, not contradictory.

## Bad patterns
| Pattern | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| "So I went international instead" | "International" isn't a verb; means nothing cold |
| Scene 1 pitches Bumpy | It's an ad immediately; relatability gone |
| Redirect assumes Bumpy knowledge | Only works if you know what Bumpy is |
| Option C invents a new concept | Must still come from the signal's emotion |
| Two scenes, same text | One scene stretched thin |
| "abroad" when a flag works | 🇧🇷 always beats "abroad" |
| Polished hook scene | Polished = ad; raw = trusted |
| An illogical line | A stranger stops believing it |

## Concept names
2–3 words, PascalCase, describing the visual mechanic of the hook, not the headline (`AirportWalk`,
`PlaneSelfie`, `BalconyStare`, `CarConfession`, `DirtyPool`, `WalkingFeet`). Keep it generic across sub-hooks that
share a code; the difference goes in the ad name's `hook` field. Check it against `core/concepts.csv` with
`python3 checks/new_concept.py check` before it goes into a brief.

## After approval → the brief
Hand to `craft/brief-writer.md`: the approved scenes and text, the persona (canonical), the audience code(s), gender
variant(s), countries or flags for destination scenes, batch, VID only or VID + IMG, V2 wanted or not, strategist
and designer codes, and the endscreen CTA for this persona.
