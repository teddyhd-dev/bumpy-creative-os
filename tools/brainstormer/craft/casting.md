# Casting: a starting point, not a rule

From the strategists' `bumpy-casting` v2.0.0 (original in `archive/strategist-files-2026-11-03/`). **Casting is
flexible:** the data below suggests who to show; the strategist decides, and the concept, the signal or a benchmark
can override it. The only hard rules are the brand rules in `core/brand.md`: never cast Russia (RU) or India (IN),
and check map frames for their pins. Data: `craft/casting-data.md`.

## How to use the data
Two kinds of signal:
- **One-sided** (attractiveness from paid swipes): what a viewer swipes right on. Desire, not necessarily returned.
- **Bilateral** (mutual matches, connections formed): both sides want it. More believable in an ad, and closer to
  what happens in the product.

When you suggest casting, prefer bilateral where it exists and fall back to one-sided when it doesn't. Say which
you used in the brainstorm (never in the designer brief).

1. **Who is watching?** The viewer segment (from the persona and language) decides which row to read; the hook
   person's look type matters more than their exact nationality.

   | Audience type | Countries |
   |---|---|
   | White / Western male | US, GB, CA, AU, DE, FR, IT, NL, SE, NO |
   | Southeast Asian female | PH, TH, ID, MY, VN |
   | Latina female | BR, CO, MX, CU, CL, AR, PE, VE |
   | Western female | US, GB, CA, AU |
   | African female | NG, KE, GH, ZA |
2. **Read that row's suggestions** in `craft/casting-data.md`.
3. **Pick 3–4 nationalities** for a profile slider (4 is the usual), one card each.
4. **Profile IDs:** rotate across briefs (don't always take the first). Give each card a generated first name:
   `Valentina (su_id 25033985) 🇧🇷`. No ID for a nationality: write a full description (name, body type, hair,
   outfit, background) so a designer can shoot without a reference; photos read as dating-app profile photos.
5. **Payoff scenes** (airport, eating, car, proposal) use one couple. The data's strongest pairs are a useful
   default: White American + Brazilian or Filipina (English-speaking audience), British + Nigerian, Turkish +
   Indonesian, Korean + Brazilian or Filipina.

Casting line in a brief:
```
[PROFILES — 4 cards: Valentina (su_id 25033985) 🇧🇷 | Angel (su_id 24969654) 🇵🇭 | Camila (su_id 25032511) 🇨🇴 | Sofía (su_id 25668990) 🇲🇽]
```

## Notes from past briefs
- For personas the viewer should see himself in (LatinaFemaleLover, PassportBro, LocalDatingFatigue men), the man is
  an everyday, average-build, slightly imperfect guy, not a model. Say it once in the casting notes.
- Make it look real: natural lighting, no white walls, tweak the face from any reference (`core/brand.md`).
