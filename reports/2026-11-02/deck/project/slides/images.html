iOS ads first live since 19 Oct, same days. Purchases per 1k impressions are about level with the videos of the same batch: the sales come, the extra clicks don’t install. Not a store-page outage: Android C>I for the whole account held at 33–38% every day since 1 Oct.

This is what made C>I look broken. On iOS, the new image ads get five percent click-through but only about one install per hundred clicks, while their purchases per impression are level with the videos. So the clicks are inflated, not lost installs: the media buyer checks where those taps come from, and we read iOS images on ROAS, not on C>I.
