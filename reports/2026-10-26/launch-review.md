**How to read it**
- ROAS = adset's Meta ROAS (Singular ruler). (×1.19) = relative to its OS that week (≥1.00 beats the OS average).
- Bar: 0.40 acceptable, under 0.40 refresh, under 0.32 critical.
- iOS week of 19 Oct is incident week INC-2026-10-20 (effect to 24 Oct) ⚠.
- Week-1 read is provisional: the W40 ads started spending Wed 28 Oct, so the read covers 5 days (28 Oct – 1 Nov).
  Share = W40 ads' share of the adset's spend in those 5 days; rank = their place among concepts in the adset.
  20%+ = provisional hit. Under 20% = strike if the adset was under 0.40 the week before (turnaround), foul if it was healthy (challenger).
  Final verdict at week 4 (week of 23 Nov). About 1 in 8 quiet entries still becomes #1 later, so leave them on.
- "Before → since launch" = 21–27 Oct vs 28 Oct – 1 Nov, relative to the OS on the same days.

## What the test run found in the system
1. **The weekly exports started on Thursday 1 Oct**, so their weeks ran Thu–Wed. The daily ad file rebuilt clean Mon–Sun weeks (spend matches the old adset export to the cent); the adset × country file was spread across weeks by days (approximation).
2. **App Store proceeds stop at 18 Oct.** The engine's creative layer only runs on weeks every source covers, so the ticket queue's #1 and reign are frozen at the week of 12 Oct (e.g. PassportBro Android shows boysvmen; the real #1 now is combo). This review computes them on the ad data directly. Next upload: proceeds to 1 Nov.
3. **Code `h7km3n` is reused for a new concept.** The confirmed alias h7km3n → `a2Ef3y` (wanted) has no concept-name filter, so the engine files the new *internationalfriends* ads as *wanted*. Result: it misses them as a new concept where wanted ran before (IFF EN iOS / EN Android), and counts them as wanted elsewhere. Fix needed: either restrict the alias to concept name *wanted*, or give internationalfriends its own code (rule: new concept = new code).
4. **Variants of a concept already in the adset are not at-bats for the engine** (spec §6.1 counts new concepts). The banner and combo W40 variants therefore don't appear in `tk_atbats`; this review judges them anyway, as the team intended them as refreshes.
5. Missing columns in the ad export: 3-second plays, ThruPlays, 25–100% plays (only the Hook Rate ratio). The adset weekly export has no Ad set ID.
